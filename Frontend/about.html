About Neuro Track

A student initiative focused on using technology to support mental well-being.

Student Initiative
TECHNOLOGY FOR A CALMER TOMORROW

Making mental well-being more accessible.

Neuro Track combines self-assessments and real-time wearable data to help you understand your well-being and build healthier habits, one step at a time.

  • Understand yourself better
  • Track progress
  • Build healthier habits
  • A calmer tomorrow

Our Mission

To make mental well-being more accessible through simple, data-driven tools that empower individuals to take charge of their health.

Our Vision

A future where technology helps create a more mindful, healthier, and happier society.

Our Approach

We combine self-reported assessments with real-time data from wearable devices to provide meaningful insights and encourage positive change.

Disclaimer

Neuro Track is a student project and is intended for educational and research purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you are experiencing severe or persistent mental health issues, please seek help from a qualified healthcare professional.
